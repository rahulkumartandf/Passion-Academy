Featured Lesson: The Water Cycle

The water cycle describes how water moves on, above, and below the surface of the Earth. It involves processes like evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection.

  • Evaporation: Water turns into vapor and rises into the atmosphere.
  • Condensation: Water vapor cools and forms clouds.
  • Precipitation: Water falls as rain, snow, or hail.
  • Collection: Water gathers in rivers, lakes, and oceans.
