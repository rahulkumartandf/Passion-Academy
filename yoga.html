Basic Yoga Asanas

Tadasana (Mountain Pose)

A foundational standing pose that improves posture, balance, and calm focus.

Vrikshasana (Tree Pose)

Enhances balance and stability in the legs, and strengthens the core.

Adho Mukha Svanasana (Downward Dog)

Stretches the hamstrings, calves, and spine while strengthening the arms and legs.

Bhujangasana (Cobra Pose)

Opens the chest and shoulders, strengthens the spine, and relieves stress.

Trikonasana (Triangle Pose)

Stretches the legs, hips, and spine, and improves digestion and balance.

Setu Bandhasana (Bridge Pose)

Strengthens the back, glutes, and legs, and opens the chest and heart.

Balasana (Child's Pose)

A gentle resting pose that calms the mind and relieves tension in the body.

Padmasana (Lotus Pose)

Promotes relaxation, meditation, and flexibility in the hips and knees.
